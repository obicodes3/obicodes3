Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 14, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Arise: Daily Quest Workout ("Application") for mobile devices. The Application is a gamified RPG fitness tracker designed to help you organize workouts, log progress, and track physical stats locally and optionally sync them with your account backend.

1. Information Collection and Use

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you generate. The types of information the Application processes include:

  • Fitness and Health Data: The Application logs physical exercise metrics (reps for pushups, sit-ups, squats) and running distances (GPS-derived if location is enabled, or manual entry).
  • Body Metrics: The Application processes height and weight to calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI) class within the gamified system.
  • Nutrition and Hydration: Daily intake logs for water (ml), calories (kcal), and proteins (g) are kept to assist in leveling stats.
  • Account Credentials: If you use the optional Google Sign-In or manual register cloud-sync feature, the Application collects your public profile name, email address, and unique user identifier to link and backup your database progress securely on the server.

2. Data Storage and Control

  • Local Storage (Default): By default, all logged workouts, attributes, BMI parameters, inventory, and stats are saved locally on your device in a private SQLite database and secure SharedPreferences.
  • Cloud Backup (Optional): If you log in, your local state is uploaded and synced to your secure cloud database account. You can request deletion of your account and related data at any time by contacting our support email.
  • Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your fitness, body metrics, or personal health records to third-party ad networks or data brokers.

3. Permissions

To provide fitness features, the Application requests the following permissions:

  • Internet Access: Required to fetch ads (AdMob) and sync data backups to the cloud backend.
  • Activity Recognition: Used to track daily steps via native device sensors.
  • Location Access: Used to track outdoor running distance and speed parameters.

4. Advertising (AdMob)

The Application integrates Google AdMob SDK to serve ads. AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers and utilize cookies to personalize ads. Users can purchase the Monarch's Awakening (Ad-Free) upgrade to completely opt-out of ad displays and bypass AdMob network calls.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have questions about our practices, please contact us at: support@arisefitquest.com.
